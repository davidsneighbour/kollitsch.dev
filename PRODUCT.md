# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primarily fellow web developers arriving via search or links for a specific technical problem (Astro, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript, git, tooling), looking for a clear, correct, first-hand answer rather than a generic tutorial. Patrick's professional peers and contacts also read the site to follow his work and thinking.

## Product Purpose

KOLLITSCH.dev is Patrick Kollitsch's personal digital garden, blog, and web-development reference. It exists to record real problems he has hit and solved, and to publish his own perspective on web development, in his own voice.

## Positioning

Value comes from first-hand, tested solutions in Patrick's own voice, published as a living, evolving reference rather than a one-off polished tutorial or SEO content farm. Posts are cross-linked and updated over time as a digital garden, not archived and abandoned.

## Operating Context

Built with Astro 6 (static output), Tailwind CSS 4, and TypeScript; deployed to Netlify. Content lives as Markdown/MDX under `src/content/blog`, organised by year. `DESIGN.md` in the repository root is the binding design-token source of truth for this project (see `AGENTS.md`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site generation via Astro; no dynamic backend/user accounts.
- Blog is the primary content type; a blogroll and social links list are also maintained as content.
- `src/pages/about.md` currently exists but is a draft placeholder — no confirmed "About" copy yet.
- Strict British English and a cordial, transactional tone are binding constraints on all copy (per `AGENTS.md`).

## Brand Commitments

Name: "KOLLITSCH.dev*" (per existing `DESIGN.md`). No further brand assets, logo, or legal constraints confirmed yet.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing blog archive under `src/content/blog/` (multiple years of first-hand technical posts) is the primary evidence of the product's actual content and voice. No testimonials, case studies, or press exist and none should be fabricated. About-page copy is not yet written.

## Product Principles

1. Every post should reflect a problem Patrick actually encountered and solved — not paraphrased documentation.
2. Preserve Patrick's personal voice and POV over generic, neutral tutorial tone.
3. Treat content as a living reference: favour updating and cross-linking existing posts over one-off publish-and-forget.
4. Correctness and specificity for fellow developers outrank breadth or SEO volume.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific accessibility requirement beyond standard web accessibility practice has been established.
